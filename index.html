🌿 Astro-Flora

Automated Life-Support Greenhouse for Space Habitats

🚀 About The Project

Astro-Flora is an Arduino-powered autonomous greenhouse designed for future lunar and Martian habitats. The system monitors plant health, automates irrigation, regulates environmental conditions, and helps conserve critical resources in extreme space environments.

✨ Features

💧 Smart Irrigation

Dual-zone soil moisture monitoring and automatic watering.

🌡 Climate Control

Automatic heater and exhaust fan management.

☀ Day/Night Cycle

Grow-light automation simulating Earth conditions.

📟 Telemetry

LCD display and real-time environmental monitoring.

🚨 Safety System

Reservoir monitoring and emergency alerts.

🔒 Crew Access Logs

Entry and exit tracking for habitat management.

🛠 Hardware

  • Arduino Mega 2560
  • Soil Moisture Sensors
  • Water Pumps
  • Relay Modules
  • LCD I2C Display
  • Buzzer
  • Temperature Sensor
  • Water Level Sensor
